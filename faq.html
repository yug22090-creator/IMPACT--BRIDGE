Common Questions

Everything you need to know about joining a mission and making a global impact through Impact Bridge.

How are volunteers matched to missions?
We use a skill-based matching algorithm that analyzes your professional background, experience level, and the specific needs of the NGO. This ensures that your expertise is used where it can create the most value.
Are the volunteering opportunities paid?
Most of our missions are volunteer-based to foster radical solidarity. However, some long-term specialized roles may include stipends for living expenses and travel costs provided by the partner organization.
Is it safe to travel to mission locations?
Safety is our top priority at Impact Bridge. We only partner with vetted NGOs that have a long-standing local presence. Each mission page includes a security assessment, and we provide thorough briefings before departure.
Can I participate remotely?
Yes! Many of our technical missions (coding, design, data analysis) offer remote participation options. You can filter for 'Remote' missions in the Opportunities section.
